**English.** YA-NEC is a NEC-2 wire antenna simulator (C# translation of nec2c, checked against nec2c and EZNEC) for Windows, Linux and macOS. Download the zip for your platform from [Releases](https://github.com/ic8tem/YA-NEC/releases), extract it to a writable folder and run `YA-NEC`. The interface is in Italian with partial English (Language, top right).
